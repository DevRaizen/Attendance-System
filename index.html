01 — Context

Background
of the Study

"Attendance is one of the major factors for measuring eligibility, punctuality, and commitment to the institution."

At General Mamerto Natividad National High School, teacher attendance and punctuality are critical determinants of academic efficiency and student success. Traditional monitoring systems exist to ensure professional accountability, administrative compliance, and accurate payroll processing — attendance tracking remains one of the most critical administrative tasks a school performs.

Under the Department of Education, attendance and punctuality are treated as core professional requirements governed by both Civil Service Commission rules and specific DepEd orders. Tracking attendance is necessary, but the manual methods used to collect it often create more friction than they resolve, slowing down daily school operations rather than supporting them.

This study responds to that gap. The system is trained on a database of teacher records — name, photo, and personal details — so that attendance is captured automatically the moment a teacher arrives on campus, using facial recognition. It was designed specifically for GMNNHS to address the time this large faculty size demands: manual record-keeping that once consumed valuable hours for both teachers and administrators is replaced by a system that logs time in, time out, and absences on its own, simplifying record-keeping and optimizing operational efficiency across the institution.
